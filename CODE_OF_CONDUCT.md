# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **WattWise** a harassment-free and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, operating system preference, gender identity, disability, ethnicity, or nationality.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Welcoming newcomers and helping first-time open-source contributors succeed
* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and constructive technical feedback
* Respecting differing viewpoints, hardware setups, and design experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
* Trolling or derailing technical discussions
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a maintainer contact issue or reaching out to the repository owner on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed fairly and promptly.
